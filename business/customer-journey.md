# Customer Journey Map for Dev-Amplify

## 1. Aware
- **Trigger Event:** Developers encounter challenges in their projects due to lack of advanced skills/tools.
- **Friction Points:** Overwhelmed by the number of tools available; unclear about which tools will genuinely enhance their skills.
- **User Emotions:** Frustration, confusion, curiosity.
- **Opportunities to Delight:** Provide educational content (webinars, blogs) that highlights the benefits of advanced skills/tools; create clear comparisons with existing tools.
- **Metric:** Number of website visits and engagement with educational content.

## 2. Consider
- **Trigger Event:** Developers start researching solutions to their skill/tool gaps.
- **Friction Points:** Difficulty in finding trustworthy reviews; skepticism about the effectiveness of new tools.
- **User Emotions:** Hopeful, cautious, analytical.
- **Opportunities to Delight:** Offer free trials or demos; showcase testimonials and case studies from other developers who have successfully used the platform.
- **Metric:** Number of demo requests and trial sign-ups.

## 3. Try
- **Trigger Event:** Developers sign up for a trial or demo of Dev-Amplify.
- **Friction Points:** Initial setup complexity; lack of immediate support during onboarding.
- **User Emotions:** Excitement mixed with anxiety; eager to see results.
- **Opportunities to Delight:** Provide a guided onboarding experience; offer live chat support during the trial period.
- **Metric:** Trial activation rate and user engagement during the trial.

## 4. Adopt
- **Trigger Event:** Developers successfully integrate Dev-Amplify into their workflow.
- **Friction Points:** Learning curve associated with new tools; potential integration issues with existing systems.
- **User Emotions:** Satisfaction, relief, empowerment.
- **Opportunities to Delight:** Regular check-ins to ensure successful integration; provide advanced tutorials and resources to maximize tool usage.
- **Metric:** Conversion rate from trial to paid subscription.

## 5. Expand
- **Trigger Event:** Developers begin to explore additional features or tools within Dev-Amplify.
- **Friction Points:** Unawareness of all available features; potential budget constraints for additional tools.
- **User Emotions:** Enthusiasm, curiosity, hesitation.
- **Opportunities to Delight:** Personalized recommendations based on usage; loyalty rewards or discounts for upgrading to premium features.
- **Metric:** Upsell conversion rate and customer lifetime value (CLV).
